# B129A CANnify Micro HV Pico-Lock · pins

Every MCU pin of the STM32G0B1CET6 (U1, LQFP48) (B129A (rev A)): where it is on the headers, what else sits on its net, and whether an application may use it. Roles: **free** reaches the headers and nothing else; **shared** has a board job by default that a solder bridge or jumper removes; **taken** is the debug port, reset or wired to a part of the board for good; **function** is a crystal, VBAT or VREF pin.
